A desk phone that brings its own tunnel.

SIPDesk registers one SIP extension and carries the call through a Tailscale userspace tunnel. The phone system stays on its office address. No separate VPN app.

Download SIPDesk.apk 87 MB · arm64-v8a and armeabi-v7a · debug build

Install

  1. Download the APK on the phone, or copy it over USB.
  2. Open it and allow installation from that source if Android asks.
  3. Allow the microphone, notifications, and full-screen alerts.
  4. Allow the VPN prompt. The phone will not register until the tunnel has a 100.x address.

First launch

Headscale serverThe URL of your Headscale server. It is not built into the app.
Auth keyA pre-auth key from that server. Later launches reuse the saved node.
ServerThe phone system’s office address, such as 192.168.2.104.
ExtensionThe SIP extension and its password.

A computer already on the tailnet at the office must share the office LAN into the tailnet. The phone system does not run Tailscale and does not get its own 100.x address.
